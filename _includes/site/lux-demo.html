Try it: the kinematics behind the energy scale

Drag the two interaction vertices to change the neutron scattering angle. The diagram calculates recoil energy from the angle and the 2.45 MeV incident neutron energy using two-body elastic scattering kinematics. Small angles correspond to recoil energies below 1 keV; a 180-degree backscatter gives the maximum energy of about 74 keV.

Drag scatter 1 along the beam line and scatter 2 within the volume. The marker on the curve follows the selected geometry.
