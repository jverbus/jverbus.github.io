Model and controls

The start radius is 1 and the target radius is 1.6. Distance, velocity and simulation time are normalized with GM = 1. Success requires both the near point (periapsis) and far point (apoapsis) to lie between 1.568 and 1.632, so a thrust-free orbit remains inside the target band.

Hohmann applies two impulses with total Δv ≈ 0.207. Greedy adjusts the orbit using small feedback impulses and plays at 4× speed. A manual burn takes over the current flight; its total includes any earlier automated burns. Tap a burn button or use Enter or Space for one impulse; hold with a pointer for repeated impulses.

This browser simulator applies impulses along or opposite the velocity, which can include a radial component. The PPO notebook instead applies impulses along the local orbital tangent. PPO training and policy rollouts are provided in the notebook.
