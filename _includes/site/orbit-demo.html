About the model

This two-body model uses normalized units with GM = 1 and orbit radii 1 and 1.6. Browser burns follow or oppose velocity, including its radial component; the PPO notebook applies burns along the local orbital tangent. Hohmann and Greedy here are analytical and feedback controllers.
